Why the name

A category, not a brand.

Instantly understood

The word is widely used, in English and well beyond it, as shorthand for Hindi-language popular cinema. A visitor needs no explanation of what to expect.

Room for every format

Streaming, tickets, news, fan communities and film heritage can all live under one name, with one audience to speak to.

.com, and the whole word

A single, short, memorable word on the most trusted extension. Easy to say aloud, easy to type, easy to remember.

Buyer diligence: a generic or descriptive term may still be covered by third-party trademark claims in some uses. Take your own legal advice before launching a commercial service. This concept site uses no studio names or marks.
