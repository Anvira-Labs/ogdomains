03 / Side project · 2026

DroneCraft Rides: video automation for an adventure-tourism operator

Architect and sole engineer.

The problem

Designed and shipped, end-to-end, a platform that automates turning raw multi-camera rafting footage into a finished customer highlight video. It replaces a manual editing step for a business whose product is the experience and its footage.

Architecture

Designed a three-part system (staff web app, Node.js API and worker, Python video service) with clear contracts between the parts. The video work runs on its own, so it can be scaled or later given smarter clip selection without changing anything else.

Simple, reliable building blocks

Chose building blocks to suit a small business: job queues run on MongoDB and SQLite instead of extra queueing software, and there is no container setup. Jobs pick up where they left off after a crash, and services confirm each step with signed callbacks. One person can run it on a couple of EC2 servers.

Built for messy real-world input

Staff-named Drive folders are matched even with typos, a missing camera doesn't block the rest, and failures can be retried per camera. Output is broadcast-consistent, in vertical and horizontal formats, with a title card that handles both English and Hindi.

Idea to deployed prototype in under a week

Code, tests, AWS infrastructure (S3, SES, EC2, nginx, pm2) and operations guides.
