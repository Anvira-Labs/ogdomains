The name

A name that says whose doorway this is

“Hindu” says the tradition. The second half of the name points at the temple: the place where daily worship, festivals and giving happen. Put together, it describes a home for temple-centred devotional services, whether that is discovery, booking, donation or community.

The name is short, easy to say aloud, and a .com, which suits a product that parents, grandparents and diaspora family may all need to type from memory.

About the spelling. The domain is spelled “Hindutample”, with an “a”. That is not the standard English spelling of “temple”. We offer it as a coined brand name. It will not match searches for the ordinary word, and some people will read it as a typo. A buyer should weigh that honestly: it is distinctive and ownable, and it needs a brand to give it meaning.

Design principle

Trust first, then convenience

Faith products live or die on trust. A buyer of this name would earn it by partnering with temples, being clear about where every rupee goes, and keeping the interface calm and respectful.

  • Temple-approved content and consent for every listing
  • Donations routed to the temple trust, with plain accounting
  • No sacred symbols on buttons, prices or anything underfoot
  • Sound, if any, off until the visitor turns it on
